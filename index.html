THE STUDIO

Built in Nigeria.
Built for the world.

Irete Labs is an independent game studio operating out of Ibadan, Nigeria, founded on a single conviction: the most powerful games come from the places and philosophies that most studios never explore.

We are not chasing trends. We are not competing on graphics budgets. We compete on something far more durable — depth of experience, cultural specificity, and the kind of creative boldness that only comes from building with nothing but vision and craft.

We are currently one person. We intend to become much more.

CULTURAL ROOTS

We draw from Yoruba tradition, Ifa philosophy, and the full breadth of African storytelling. These are not decorations — they are the core design material.

PRIMAL DESIGN

Every game we make is designed to touch something before the graphics, before the mechanics — something in the player that recognizes truth when it feels it.

THE LONG GAME

We are not building for the next quarter. We are building a studio that will still be here, and still matter, in thirty years.

BUILD LOG

The build,
in public.

A devlog — a development log — is how we document the studio being built in real time. Not press releases. Not polished trailers. The actual process: the decisions, the breakthroughs, the failures, and the lessons between them.

Every game we make, you will watch us make it. Every studio hire, you will see why they were chosen. This is not transparency as strategy — it is a commitment to everyone who will come to build alongside us.
